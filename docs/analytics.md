# Business Analytics

The warehouse was designed to answer business questions related to lending portfolio performance and credit risk.

## Portfolio

1. What is the total number of loans?
2. What is the total loan amount issued?
3. What is the total amount funded?
4. What is the average loan amount?
5. What is the average interest rate?

## Credit Risk

6. What is the loan-status distribution?
7. What is the overall charge-off rate?
8. What is the charge-off rate by loan grade?
9. What is the loan volume by loan grade?
10. What is the charge-off rate by FICO band?
11. What is the charge-off rate by DTI band?
12. What is the charge-off rate by verification status?
13. What is the charge-off rate by home ownership?
14. Which loan purposes have the highest charge-off rates?

## Portfolio Performance

15. What is the total principal received?
16. What is the total interest received?
17. What is the total outstanding principal?
18. Which loan grades have the highest outstanding principal?

## Geography and Trends

19. Which states have the highest funded amount?
20. How has loan origination and charge-off rate changed by year?

## Important Analytical Consideration

Charge-off rates by loan issue year should be interpreted carefully.

Recent loan cohorts have had less time to mature and therefore may not yet have experienced the same amount of charge-off activity as older cohorts.
